Bundled reader fonts

Font binaries come from the pinned upstream revisions and source SHA-256 hashes
in manifest.json. Google Fonts and OpenDyslexic are distributed under the SIL
Open Font License; each family license is included beside its files.

Upstream font binaries were converted to WOFF2 with fontTools and Brotli without
subsetting glyphs or changing font designs. Only the chosen family is embedded
in the EPUB document. No font downloads occur while using the app.

OpenDyslexic is an optional typeface designed for readers with dyslexia. Font
preferences differ; this app makes no claim that a typeface treats dyslexia.
